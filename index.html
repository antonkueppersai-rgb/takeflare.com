Why the phone is quiet

Good businesses get missed for small, fixable reasons.

The work is good and the reviews are kind. Still, new people don't find you. It's usually one of these:

  • 01The Google profile is half filled in, so you don't show up when someone nearby searches.
  • 02There's no website, or there's one nobody has touched in years and it still lists last year's prices.
  • 03Instagram has a few posts with an old logo, then nothing for months.
  • 04Nobody has written down, in one clear sentence, why a customer should pick you over the place next door.

About

Questions

Before you ask.

Do you work with businesses outside Germany?

Yes. Everything runs remotely over video calls, WhatsApp or email, and shared files. Clients don't need to be in Berlin.

Which languages?

English, German and Russian, for the calls and for the posts and pages themselves.

Who owns the accounts?

You do. Your Google profile, domain and social accounts are set up in your name. I get access to do the work, and you keep the keys if we ever stop.

How long until it's all live?

Plan for about four weeks from the first call. It's faster if you already have a name and logo, and slower if we go back and forth on designs.

Do I have to post or film anything myself?

No. A few photos of your place and your work help a lot, but I write, design and publish the posts. You approve them first.

Can I start with just one piece?

Sure. A lot of people start with the Google profile, because that's where nearby customers look first.

Tell me about your business.

A free 20-minute call. You'll leave with at least one thing you can fix yourself the same day.
